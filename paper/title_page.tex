\section*{Statements and Declarations}
\paragraph{Funding} No funding was received for conducting this study. All models ran on the author's own hardware.

\paragraph{Competing interests} The author has no competing interests to declare that are relevant to the content of this article.

\paragraph{Ethics approval} Not applicable. This study involved no human participants and no animals. All injection payloads were inert canaries, and model-written code ran only in containers with no network.

\paragraph{Consent to participate and to publish} Not applicable.

\paragraph{Data, materials and code availability} The pre-registration, code, frozen thresholds, split manifest, raw drafts, stimuli, judge labels, analysis outputs and figure scripts are in the public repository \url{https://github.com/C4r50n-R0D/gate-cost-study} and archived in the replication package at Zenodo, \zenodo{}. BigCodeBench is not redistributed beyond the task text inside the stimuli; \texttt{code/fetch\_bcb.sh} fetches it at a pinned revision and checks its SHA-256. Model digests are given in Table 1 of the manuscript.

\paragraph{Use of AI tools} Documented in Section 3.10 of the manuscript.

\paragraph{Author contributions} Carson Rodriguez conceived and directed the study, made the pre-registered design and approval decisions, and read and approved the final manuscript. The use of AI tools in this work is documented in Section 3.10 of the manuscript.
